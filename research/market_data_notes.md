# Market data survey notes (retrieved 2026-10-13)

## Method caveat
Pages were read through a summarising fetch tool. Re-check quoted clauses verbatim before legal reliance. Text in quotation marks in the CSV was returned in quotes by the tool; other licence text is labelled paraphrase.

## Unreachable or blocked
- rawlinsons.com.au and drewry.co.uk/terms: blocked by robots.txt. Rawlinsons rests on secondary sources only; Drewry licence unconfirmed.
- fbx.freightos.com: fetch error (Terminal page used instead).
- Terms pages linked but not read: LME fees/licences, Baltic Settlement Licence and Data Policy, Freightos full T&Cs, OilPriceAPI Data Usage Policy, Trading Economics pricing/terms, Fastmarkets API articles, ABS attribution page.

## Unsure or weak evidence
- ABS: CC BY 4.0 is site-wide; the Data API guide does not reproduce licence text. PPI dataflow IDs, rate limits, hosting region unconfirmed.
- RBA: no API documented; automated retrieval position unconfirmed. "Not for commercial reliance" remark is a paraphrase.
- World Bank Pink Sheet: CC BY 4.0 is the stated default for datasets, but the Pink Sheet's own catalogue record was not read. It cites third-party sources (Bloomberg, S&P Global, Urner Barry); upstream restrictions unconfirmed. No steel or cement series in the March 2025 PDF.
- IMF: terms contain two seemingly different statements (permission needed for commercial reuse; broad permission to distribute Data). Treat as permission required.
- Cotality: Construction API pricing, fields and terms unconfirmed; whether "live supplier feeds" are list, trade or estimated prices is unconfirmed.
- Freightos: "wide audience" licensing wording is paraphrased from the Terminal page. Oceania lane unconfirmed.
- Baltic, Fastmarkets, Trading Economics: landing pages only; coverage and licence unconfirmed.
- simPRO/Reece: marketplace blurb only; no evidence of a third-party feed.

## Gaps
- No Australian steel or timber price index publisher found. ABS "Price indexes of materials used in building" appears legacy (search result only, not read).
- No Master Builders or HIA price index found.
- No public supplier price-list API found. Bunnings scrapers on Apify exist but have no confirmed licence and are not recommended.
- Pink Sheet and IMF publication lag and history start dates unconfirmed.
