## Rules
- **One pass. List every problem now;** do not hold any back for a later round.
- Class each problem **BLOCKING** (the owner should not decide until it is fixed), **SHOULD FIX**, **OPTIONAL** or **MINOR**, with a rough cost to fix (small, medium, large).
- Do not re-review draft 1, and do not widen the scope: no new features, no new insights, no production-readiness or integration design. A gap you find in the blueprint itself goes in a separate short list, labelled as such.
- Quote the text you are judging (section name and a few words) so each point can be found.
- Say "not verifiable from the pack" where the pack cannot settle a point, rather than guessing.
