Elevating the Design to "Elite" Status
Draft 3 establishes an exceptionally rigorous architectural foundation—guaranteeing tenant isolation, audit provenance, and strict separation between data supply intake and insight logic.
To take this design from robust and compliant to truly elite, several high-leverage architectural and product capabilities can be added. These target the specific operational realities, Victorian legal frameworks (such as the Building and Construction Industry Security of Payment Act 2002), and field-level behaviors of $5M–$50M commercial builders.
1. Automated Back-to-Back Subcontractor Claim Reconciliation ("Pass-Through" Defense)
 * The Gap: The draft handles client claims and subcontractor claims across separate releases, but misses the core commercial leakage vector: discrepancies between what a trade contractor claims and what the builder is simultaneously claiming from the principal client.
 * Elite Addition: Implement an automated Back-to-Back Traceability Engine within the intake layer. When a subcontractor claim arrives, APG should instantly cross-reference it against the corresponding client claim line item, flagging over-certification, unapproved trade variations passed upward without client backing, and margin squeeze before payment schedules are issued.
2. Statutory Declaration & Worker Entitlement Compliance Intake
 * The Gap: Australian commercial builders face strict statutory obligations regarding subcontractor payments and statutory declarations (Stat Decs) to ensure downstream workers and suppliers are paid.
 * Elite Addition: Add a Compliance Gatekeeper Entity to the intake pipeline. This entity ingests signed subcontractor statutory declarations alongside monthly progress claims. If a subcontractor fails to provide a verified Stat Dec confirming downstream payments, APG automatically gates payment certification and triggers a high-priority risk alert to the commercial manager.
3. One-Tap Mobile Action Loops for Site Managers & CAs (Frictionless Nudge Execution)
 * The Gap: Nudges and missing-data lists are well-designed, but busy site managers and contract administrators rarely open reporting dashboards to clear administrative backlogs.
 * Elite Addition: Architect a Zero-UI Action Protocol for nudges. Instead of requiring users to log into APG to resolve a data gap (e.g., missing cost code or unconfirmed site instruction), nudges sent via SMS, email, or Microsoft Teams/Slack should embed cryptographic One-Tap Action Tokens (e.g., [Approve Rate], [Assign Default Cost Code], [Attach Photo]). Clicking the token executes the intake validation and clears the readiness gate instantly without a web login.
4. Cryptographic "Adjudication-Ready" Evidence Export Packages
 * The Gap: Construction disputes and Security of Payment (SOP) adjudications require airtight audit trails proving when instructions were given, when variations were logged, and who verified them.
 * Elite Addition: Leverage APG's robust provenance tracking (PG-DS-AC-04) to introduce a native Dispute & Adjudication Export Feature. When a claim or variation enters dispute, APG should package the complete immutable chain of custody—original email headers, file SHA-256 hashes, timestamped photo metadata, verification logs, and intake status history—into a single, verifiable PDF/ZIP evidentiary bundle ready for legal or adjudicator review.
5. Self-Learning Chart of Accounts Crosswalk Engine
 * The Gap: The design treats the cost-code map as a static setup questionnaire, which can stall onboarding if a builder uses messy, non-standard chart-of-accounts naming conventions across different projects.
 * Elite Addition: Introduce an AI-Assisted Adaptive Crosswalk Engine that remembers historical mappings across builder projects. When a new supplier bill or export lands with an unfamiliar general ledger code, APG's ingestion layer should predict the correct standard cost code with a confidence score, requiring human confirmation only for low-confidence matches while continuously training on builder overrides.
Summary Verdict
By incorporating automated back-to-back claim matching, statutory compliance gating, zero-UI mobile action tokens, adjudication-ready evidence export packages, and adaptive cost-code learning, APG moves beyond passive data readiness tracking into an active, risk-eliminating commercial command center for Victorian builders.
