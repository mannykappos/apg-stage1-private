## 4. Problems in revision 4.1 itself

**R41 changes that do not carry what they claim to consolidate:**
- **R41-17 (E84)** claims B15, C-G-FND and D-G-FND but leaves out these parts of the S06 s6 finding contract: explanation, financial interpretation, severity and urgency held separately, reviewer and next review date, deduplication identity, assertion-basis label, suspected/confirmed relationships, and the late/missed marker. Together these cause 26 of the 81 gaps. This is the biggest shortfall.
- **R41-19 (E86)** claims C-G-REC, but "lifecycle (proposed to superseded or invalidated)" names none of the S10 s9 states and no blocked/referred state. The eligibility-gate result and the constraints are both missing.
- **R41-27 (E46)** claims C-G-SCN, but C-G-SCN asked for exactly two things, affected activities/resources and required approvals, and R41-27 has neither.
- **R41-34 (E30)** claims C-G-CMP, but "comparable or not" is a yes/no where S08 s5 needs five classes, and it has no documented adjustments.
- **R41-20 (E88)** claims D-G-SAV but leaves out the offsetting/hidden costs and the verifier.
- **R41-24b (E44)**: "the savings-baseline contents (S10 s9, S13)" names no fields. It is vague wording, not a field group. It also cites S10 s9, which is the approval boundaries; the savings baseline is S05 s10.1 and S13 s13.6.
- **R41-03 (E02)** claims B5 and D-G-INT but leaves out the duplicate/retry/replay policy, owner and escalation contact, failure class, retry schedule, missing coverage and required intervention. "Integration status: last success, failures, sync state" is thinner than D-G-INT.
- **R41-04 (E03)** claims B6 but leaves out the handling of revisions, cancellations, late records and corrections, and the conflict outcomes with their resolver role (both S04 s5).
- **R41-05 (E103)** claims B2 but has no retry or replay history.
- **R41-09 (E35)** claims B8 but has no "evidence considered" field, and its disposition is only confirmed or corrected.
- **R41-10 (E96)** claims B9 but adds no "risk-adjusted" value status (S05 C0066).

**Class and platform markings:**
