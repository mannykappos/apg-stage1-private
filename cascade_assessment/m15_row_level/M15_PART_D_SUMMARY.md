How rows were treated: build-programme artefacts (the RTM, the DB-Dependent Closure Register, implementation job records, milestone evidence packs, test fixtures and test evidence artefacts, change-control records for the blueprint) are held in the delivery repository, not in APG, so they are NO-DATA-NEED. ## Proposed fixes (smallest change for each gap)

- **G-AUD** (18 rows): new entity Audit log entry (tenant, actor identity and role, action/event type, subject kind and id, state or value before and after, reason, evidence ref, timestamp, correlation id, outcome, tamper-evidence sequence/hash)
- **G-IAM** (15 rows): field groups on E05 (identity type human/integration/service/automated; identity status active/disabled/suspended/expired/revoked with dates; MFA requirement and enrolment; session expiry/revocation unless held by the Anchor identity service; role permission sets read/create/update/approve/export/administer; role assignments scoped to organisation, project or resource; time-bounded privileged grants with grantor, expiry, revocation)
- **G-TEN** (1 rows): E01 gains the owning tenant (organisation) id on every record, so isolation does not rest on a client-supplied id or an indirect project link
- **G-CLS** (7 rows): new configuration entity Data classification and retention policy (per data category: classification level, purpose, permitted users/services/integrations, retention rule, archival/deletion rule, legal-hold rule, export/audit restrictions) plus E01 field group (classification level, preservation/legal hold flag with reason, set by and date)
- **G-INT** (6 rows): field groups on E02 (integration scopes/permissions, credential reference and status with rotation/revocation dates, never the secret; last successful sync; import completeness complete/partial with counts; failure class; retry schedule; missing coverage; required intervention and owner)
- **G-JOB** (9 rows): new entity Background operation and external effect (operation type, tenant, trigger and correlation id, payload ref, lifecycle state pending/running/succeeded/failed/retry-scheduled/dead-lettered/interrupted/outcome-unknown, attempts, failure class, next retry, idempotency key, inbound event id and receipt order, external reference, reconciled outcome and evidence, dead-letter owner, replay and closure)
- **G-FND** (4 rows): field groups on E84 (stable finding identity/deduplication key, lifecycle state and history open/downgraded/closed/reopened/superseded, reopen reason, owner, relationships to linked findings and shared event/root cause, overlap/double-counting group)
- **G-APR** (3 rows): field groups on E87 (proposed action, risks considered, approving authority role/limit, resulting state; emergency override: eligibility basis, reason, authoriser, subsequent review by/date/result)
- **G-SAV** (1 rows): field group on E88 (approved versioned savings baseline ref, implementation evidence, offsetting/hidden costs, net realised result, verified by and date)
- **G-AI** (1 rows): new entity AI interaction record (tenant, user, request, retrieval refs, output, model/version, verification status, retention class), or a register decision that APG retains no AI context/embeddings
- **G-OPS** (5 rows): new entity Operational record (component/data-domain state transitions with entry/exit, affected capabilities, last successful operation; release records; restoration-test records)
- **G-INC** (10 rows): new entity Incident, security or operational (detected/start time, components, data and tenants affected, severity, suspected/confirmed status, cause state suspected/confirmed/unresolved, evidence refs, escalation and notification decisions, recovery, integrity sign-off, post-incident review, corrective actions with owners and due dates)

G-OPS and G-INC are platform-operations records. They are needed by the text (S15 15.12, S16 s3, s8, s10, s11), but the owner may decide they live in operations tooling rather than in APG's data store. Either way the register should record the decision; until then they are gaps.

## What I think is wrong in the M15 heading-level result

1. **Sections 14 to 17 were set aside wholesale** as governing "the build, tests and operations rather than data". That is right for Sections 14 and 17 (with a handful of restatements, all covered except the finding-lifecycle and realised-saving gaps below), but wrong for Sections 15 and 16. Those sections state product data APG must keep: an audit log (S15 15.8, S16 s12), identities, permissions and time-bounded privileged access (S15 15.3, 15.4), a tenant key on every record (S15 15.5), data classification, retention and legal hold (S15 15.6), integration connection, scope and sync status (S15 15.10, S16 s9), background operation and unknown-outcome state (S16 s6), approval and override detail (S15 15.11), and operational and incident records (S15 15.12, S16 s3, s8, s10, s11). None of these is in the register.

2. **No audit log entity.** The audit trail is required not only by S15 to S16 but by earlier locked sections the heading check did read (S01 Gate 01-C, S04 s17 and PG-DI-AC-15, S06 implementation, S12 state transitions and s9). E01 revision and supersession is record history, not an audit trail of who did what and why. This is the most significant omission.

3. **E84 Finding lacks its lifecycle and relationships.** S06 (decision 2 and 3, s6 "relationships: linked findings, root-cause hypotheses and overlap/double-counting groups", and the implementation line "stable finding identity, deduplication, relationships, lifecycle, ownership and audit history") is not carried by E84's field group (rule, evidence, quantification, confidence, limits). The heading-level check lists S06 as checked but did not raise it. Part D raises it at S14 L0056, L0073, L0091, C0093; the Part covering S06 should find the source rows.

4. **E88 Outcome cannot hold realised-saving verification.** S05 s10.1 (and S08, S09, S13 checkpoints) require an approved versioned baseline, implementation evidence, offsetting costs and authorised verification. E88 holds decision, realised result and date. Raised here at S14 L0069 (G-SAV).

5. **E87 Decision/approval lacks risks, approving authority, resulting state and override fields** required by S15 15.11 (G-APR).

6. The statement that "none affects the first data release" should be re-checked for the audit log, identity/permission and tenant-key gaps: these are foundation controls (S14 Phase 1, Gate 1) rather than later insights.

7. Minor: the method says it covered Sections 00 to 17, so Section 18 was not checked at heading level (not in Part D; noted only).
