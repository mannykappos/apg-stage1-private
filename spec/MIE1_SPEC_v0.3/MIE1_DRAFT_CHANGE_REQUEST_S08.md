- Current wording/reference: s8 "Wait: Assess explicit future-price scenarios, uncertainty, availability, lead-time changes, delay exposure and incremental later-purchase costs." and "Do not apply a universal rule that prices will rise or fall. Lowest-price scenarios are not automatically best." s7: market observations are benchmarking and history; s7 says historical trends do not guarantee future prices. The locked text therefore gives observed market movement no defined role in the buy-timing outcome beyond benchmarking and history. M6 follows this: index history "feeds no calculation" (apg/mkt/scenarios.py:11).
- Proposed amendment: two options; the product owner chooses one, or neither.
  - Option A — context only. Add to s8: "Market-movement signals derived from licensed, fresh observations that retain source, date, validity, scope and limitations may be shown beside the buy-now, wait, stage and negotiate scenarios as labelled context. They are observed facts, not forecasts. They do not select, rank, weight or alter any scenario or outcome. Wait continues to rest on explicit future-price scenarios." Add to s14: "A displayed market signal retains the fields required of a market-dependent finding and is labelled historical or current." Effect: the engine's signals are visible to the user; the timing outcome is unchanged. The APG pack stays CONTEXT_ONLY.
  - Option B — context plus assumption-labelled owner rules. Option A, plus add to s8: "A timing assessment may use an observed market signal as one input only through a rule that (a) is configuration scoped to a named item class and market, versioned and approved by the named policy owner; (b) states the owner's assumption in words; (c) is labelled assumption-based in the output together with the signal, its evidence, data age and limitations; (d) is never presented as a forecast or as a universal tendency of prices; (e) is qualified or withheld when required evidence is stale, expired, unavailable or conflicting; (f) never alters the authoritative forecast; and (g) leaves purchase commitment and material substitution to human approval; and (h) applies to every timing outcome that a market signal influences, whether ACT_NOW, WAIT, STAGE, NEGOTIATE or HOLD, and to any outcome reached only because such a rule did not match, which includes every rule placed after such a rule whatever that earlier rule's own outcome (NOT_ASSESSED included) (v0.2 clarification: the safeguard is not limited to WAIT and HOLD; v0.3 clarification: no later rule is exempt because an earlier signal-influenced rule has the outcome NOT_ASSESSED)." Effect: the APG pack may use the SIGNAL_RULES profile with owner-supplied rules; every outcome a market signal influences is assumption-labelled and discloses its influence class (MIE-R-63); the builder supplies no rule or threshold.
  - Both options keep, unchanged: no unqualified forecast; no universal assumption that prices rise or fall; scenarios cannot alter approved forecasts; a human decides every purchase.
  - New acceptance criteria would be added by change control; identifiers are not assigned here. Intent: signals never reach an unqualified current recommendation when evidence is stale; signal-derived inputs are labelled; no signal-derived input alters the authoritative forecast; no outcome is produced without a pack-owner rule (Option B) or at all (Option A).
- Rationale and supporting evidence: the product owner has ruled that the Market Intelligence Engine is to track market movement and tell the user when timing is favourable; Section 08 as locked supports only scenario-based timing. M6 contains no market-movement engine (procurement analysis only; see MIE-1 specification s1 and s14). The core specified in MIE1_SPEC.md already enforces the four safeguards by construction (MIE-R-16, -19, -20, -25, -26, -27), so the change request is needed only to permit APG to use signals, not to relax any safeguard.
