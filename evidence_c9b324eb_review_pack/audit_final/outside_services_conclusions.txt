   extraction of freeze c9b324eb (auditor_env_names: the five names).

   Secret-bearing names were recorded by name only, as the tool does, but the values of other session identifiers were recorded. That
   record was read only by the builder and the mapping agent inside this workspace, was never sent anywhere, has been
   deleted, and is not evidence. It was found by the independent mapping agent (its discrepancies 18

 - The audit tool starts its subprocess child with subprocess.run, as the 11-05 tool did. It does NOT start it through this freeze's
   run_unit (the Popen hook, stdin /dev/null), and its forked child's scrub is the tool's own, not run_forked. So this audit shows what
   an outside service offers a unit; it is not evidence of AM-4 or of the P.2 scrub. Those are shown by the unit tests
   (test_no_timeout_kill.py P2SubprocessUnits::test_what_a_unit_child_inherits, P2ForkedUnits::test_what_a_forked_child_inherits).
